# AI Trading Room

Open trading research built with AI. Every idea is coded, backtested on real broker data and published here with the honest result, **including the ones that failed.**

New research is posted one topic at a time. Each post has its own folder with the code, a plain-language write-up and the result.

> **Disclaimer:** research and education only. Nothing here is financial advice or a signal service. Backtests are not live results, past performance does not predict future returns, and trading leveraged products can lose more than you deposit.
