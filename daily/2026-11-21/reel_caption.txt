$5,000 → $3.52 million. On paper. Here's why it's fake. 👀

Our own 5-market portfolio backtest at 1% risk (2021–2026) printed $3.52M. Why we don't believe it:
• Lot cap: positions hit the 100-lot broker max, so growth stalls in later years
• Slippage: a backtest fills 100 lots at the quoted price — real markets don't
• Selection bias: the markets were chosen after seeing the results
• At 2% risk the max drawdown was 58% — more than half the account gone at one point

Check instead: first-year return, max drawdown, worst month, % of months positive, out-of-sample period.
