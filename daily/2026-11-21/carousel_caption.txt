We coded "MAP Trend Rider" and ran it in the MT5 Strategy Tester, 2005–2026:
• Grid of round-number "MAP" price levels (spacing ≥ 1.5 × ATR)
• Daily close beyond a level in the 200-day SMA direction → enter next open
• Stop one level back, trailed level by level, no fixed target, 1% risk
• Balance ≈ $10k → peak ≈ $13k (2014) → ≈ $3k (2026)
• Lesson: one good decade can hide a long, slow bleed
