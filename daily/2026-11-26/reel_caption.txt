We ran the New York opening range breakout on forex (EURUSD, GBPUSD) and split the test in two: train 2021–2024, test 2025–2026.

• EURUSD: +97.5R in-sample → −36.6R out-of-sample
• GBPUSD: +3.7R → −54.0R
• Our NY ORB on US500/NAS100 stayed profitable in walk-forward checks — that edge is tied to indices at the NY cash open, not forex
• On forex, the London open mattered more than New York

A result that only works in one period is not an edge. Always test on data you didn't tune on.
