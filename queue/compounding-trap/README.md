# Why "$5k → $3.5 million" backtests lie

Our own portfolio test at 1% risk turned $5,000 into $3.52 million on paper (2021–2026). Why that number is not real:
1. **Max lot size:** in later years positions hit the broker cap (100 lots), so growth stalls — 2025–2026 returns fall at high risk.
2. **Liquidity and slippage:** a backtest fills 100 lots at the quoted price; real markets don't.
3. **Selection bias:** the markets were chosen after seeing the results.
4. **Drawdown in percent hides money:** at 2% risk the max drawdown was 58% — more than half the account gone at one point.

**What to look at instead:** first-year return, max drawdown, worst month, % of months positive, and an out-of-sample period.
